This project is being completed in an academic context. Therefore, all agentic programming and AI usage must comply with the academic-integrity policies of the University of Calgary. For this SENG 533 course, the following AI Integrity Policy ensures that AI usage is transparent, responsible, and consistent with academic standards.

Course Specific [0]:
- **Transparent usage.** AI may be used for content creation and learning, but it must be properly acknowledged.
- **Evaluation and revision.** Drafting, problem-solving, and code assistance are allowed, but the resulting work must be evaluated and revised until the developers are confident in its accuracy and quality.
- **Documented usage.** Prompts, tool identification (model, version, and tool), and outputs must be documented and summarized.

General AI Integrity Policy:
- AI should support learning and thought processes, not replace them. It should enhance understanding and development rather than substitute for personal effort and critical thinking.
- All AI usage must be documented in commit messages. Each commit involving AI assistance must include a clear statement describing the nature of the AI's contribution. See the [commit-message skill](./.github/skills/commit-message/SKILL.md) for the required format.
- Developers must review and verify all AI-generated content. They must provide an exact, developer-written validation statement for the commit message that describes the meaningful checks they performed and their results. Agents must not write, rewrite, summarize, infer, or substitute their own validation wording. Validation must occur before AI-assisted changes are pushed and must be more than a superficial check.
- Agents may generate code, but developers must understand the generated code and be able to explain its functionality and implications. Developers must not blindly accept AI-generated work.
- AI usage documentation must include concise summaries of the input prompts and outputs used, along with the AI tool, model, version, and usage context (for example, Copilot CLI or web).

These requirements apply whether AI is used for writing, analysis, design, research, code generation, debugging, or editing.
